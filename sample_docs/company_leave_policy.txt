# Apex Global Technologies — Employee Leave & Benefits Policy 2026

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2.08 days per month. A maximum of 5 unused days may be rolled over into the following calendar year, expiring on March 31st.

## 2. Sick & Medical Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year upon joining. Medical leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires a certified doctor's note submitted to the HR Portal within 48 hours of return.

## 3. Remote Work & Work From Anywhere
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. The company also offers a "Work from Anywhere" program allowing up to 30 continuous calendar days of international remote work per year, subject to data security clearance and timezone overlap requirements (minimum 4 hours overlapping with UTC-5).

## 4. Parental & Caregiver Leave
- Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Parental leave must be utilized within the first 12 months following childbirth or legal adoption.

## 5. Professional Learning Stipend
Each employee is allocated an annual learning budget of $1,500 for professional certifications, books, and technical conferences. Reimbursement requests must be submitted through Expensify with itemized invoices.
